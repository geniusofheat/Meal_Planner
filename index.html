Welcome to Meal Planner

Find recipes, plan every meal and build your grocery list, all in one app.

Free Version Tools :

These tools are included with your free account. Tap a tool title to read what it does.

  1. Grocery List :

    Build your grocery list by typing an item name or speaking into the microphone (🎤).

    • Add items by typing or speaking
    • Remove an item with the ✕ button
    • Check an item off when you take it from the shelf
    • Lock button locks your selections
  2. Recipe Menu :

    See the recipe menu and preview one free recipe.

    • Browse the recipe menu
    • Open one free recipe to preview it
    • Upgrade to open the complete list of recipes

Full Version :

Upgrade

To use every tool described below, you need to upgrade to the full version. The full version unlocks the complete recipe menu, the Calendar, Favorite Recipes, My Recipes, the Notepad and the Timer and Alarm, and includes all future upgrades. Tap a tool title to read what it does.

  1. Recipe Menu :

    Browse hundreds of chef-inspired recipes sorted and alphabetized into categories.

    • Recipe Finder: the search bar finds recipes by name or ingredient and searches every category at once, with matches highlighted in the menu
    • Search bar to find a recipe by name
    • Microphone (🎤) to search by voice
    • Categories sorted alphabetically
    • Tap a category to open its subtitles, then tap a subtitle to see its recipes
    • Recipes listed alphabetically in every subtitle
    • Back button returns you to your place in the list
    • Recipe cards: each recipe opens as its own card with the ingredients list, servings, preparation instructions, a Save button (adds it to your favorites), a Plan button (sends it to the meal planner) and a Back button
  2. Grocery List :

    Build and keep your shopping list.

    • Add items by typing or speaking into the microphone (🎤)
    • Remove an item with the ✕ button
    • Check items as you shop
    • Lock button locks your selections
    • Browse the grocery menu and select multiple items without typing
    • Search the grocery menu by typing or speaking
    • Save the list and view your saved lists
    • Pin the list to a day in the calendar
  3. Calendar

    Plan meals and pin events to any day of the month.

    • Plan button on each recipe card
    • Choose a day on the monthly calendar
    • Plan Events button pins an event title to a date
    • Days with something planned are highlighted
    • Seven-day list under the calendar
    • Each planned meal shows its ingredients
    • Each planned meal shows its preparation instructions
    • Plan favorite recipes and your own custom recipes
  4. My Recipes :

    Create a recipe card with the same layout as the recipes in the recipe menu.

    • Add a recipe with the [ + ] button
    • Use your own recipe cards in the meal plan
  5. Favorite Recipes :

    Keep the recipes you love together in one place.

    • Press the save button on any recipe card
    • Saved recipes are added to your favorites list
    • All saved recipes in one place, no digging through categories
  6. Notepad :

    Keep notes in lists, with formatting, photos and voice typing.

    See screenshots
    • Make a list for each subject and add notes to it
    • Format text with bold, italic, colors, lists and checkboxes
    • Type by voice and add photos
    • Your notes are saved to your account and work on every device
  7. Timer and Alarm :

    A digital timer and an alarm clock in one tool.

    • Timer: set hours, minutes and seconds, or tap a preset like 20 minutes, and watch the digital clock count down
    • Pause, resume or reset the timer at any time
    • Alarm: pick a time, add a label and turn each alarm on or off
    • Rings with sound and vibration, with a snooze button

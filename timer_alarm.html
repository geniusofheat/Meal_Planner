    Help

    • Timer: set hours (up to 99), minutes and seconds, or tap a preset, then press Start. Press ▲ to double the time or ▼ to halve it.
    • Press Pause to stop it, Resume to continue, and Reset to start the same time over. Clear sets the timer back to zero.
    • Alarm: pick a time, add an optional label and press Add.
    • Use the switch beside an alarm to turn it on or off, and the ✕ to delete it.
    • When a timer or alarm rings, press Stop, or Snooze to ring again in 5 minutes.
    • Keep this page open so it can ring.

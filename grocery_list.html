Adding items. Tap Add, type what you need, then tap Done. Each item is placed in the correct category for you, and you can check it off once it is in your cart.

Locking your list. Tap Lock when you are ready to shop. A locked list cannot have items added or removed by accident, but you can still check things off. Tap Unlock to make changes again.

Using the menu. Tap Grocery Menu to see common items grouped by category. Tap any item to put it on your list, and tap Grocery Menu again to close it.

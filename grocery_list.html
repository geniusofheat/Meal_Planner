Check the items you want, press Save on that category, then press the Back button at the top to return to your grocery list.

Adding items. Type what you need in the box, then tap Add. Each item is placed in the correct category for you, and you can check it off once it is in your cart.

Locking your list. Tap Lock when you are ready to shop. A locked list cannot have items added or removed by accident, but you can still check things off. Tap Unlock to make changes again.

Using the menu. Tap Grocery Menu to see common items grouped by category. Check the items you want and tap Save on that category to put them on your list, then tap Back to return here.

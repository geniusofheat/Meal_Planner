📖 Cookbook Features :

Open !

All of the tools listed below are contained within the cookbook. Open the cookbook to access them.

Tap a subtitle below to review its content.

  1. Recipe Menu :

    Hundreds of chef-inspired recipes sorted and alphabetized into categories. Each recipe opens as its own card so you keep your place in the menu.

    • Ingredients list
    • Servings
    • Preparation instructions
    • Save button (adds the recipe to your favorites)
    • Plan button (sends the recipe to the meal planner)
    • Back button (returns you to your place in the list)
  2. Grocery List :

    Build and keep your shopping list.

    • Add items by typing or speaking into the microphone (🎤)
    • Remove an item with the ✕ button
    • Check items as you shop
    • Lock button locks your selections
    • Browse the grocery menu and select multiple items without typing
    • Search the grocery menu by typing or speaking
    • Save the list and view your saved lists
    • Pin the list to a day in the meal planner
  3. Meal Planner :

    Plan a different meal for every day of the month.

    • Plan button on each recipe card
    • Choose a day on the monthly calendar
    • Planned days are highlighted orange
    • Seven-day list under the calendar
    • Each planned meal shows its ingredients
    • Each planned meal shows its preparation instructions
    • Plan favorite recipes and your own custom recipes
  4. Recipe Creator :

    Create a recipe card with the same layout as the cookbook recipes.

    • Add a recipe with the [ + ] button
    • Use your own recipe cards in the meal plan
  5. Recipe Finder :

    The smart search tool finds recipes in the cookbook.

    • Search by recipe name
    • Search by ingredient
    • Type into the text box or speak into the microphone (🎤)
    • Every category is searched at once
    • Matching recipes are highlighted in the menu
  6. Favorite Recipes :

    Keep the recipes you love together in one place.

    • Press the save button on any recipe card
    • Saved recipes are added to your favorites list
    • All saved recipes in one place, no digging through categories

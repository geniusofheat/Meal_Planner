Full Version Tools :

  1. 📖 Cookbook :

    Open !

    All of the tools listed below are contained within the cookbook. Open the cookbook to access them.

    Tap the cookbook feature title you want to explore or expand, and the information will be provided below it.

    Features :

    • Recipe Cards :

      You have access to hundreds of chef-inspired recipes sorted and alphabetized into categories. Each recipe card opens in its own popup window so you can keep your place in the menu.

    • Favorite Recipes :

      Add recipes to your favorites list by pressing the star button. All of your saved recipes live together in one place, no digging through categories.

    • Meal Planner :

      Use recipes with the meal planner. Press the plan button within each recipe card, then choose a day on the monthly calendar.

    • Recipe Creator :

      Recipe creator lets you create a recipe card with the same cookbook recipe layout. Accessible only in the cookbook.

    • Recipe Finder :

      The smart search tool lets you search recipes by name or ingredients. Use the text box or the microphone to search the cookbook.

    • Grocery List :

      Build your grocery list by typing an item name or speaking into the microphone(🎤). Check items as you shop and press the lock button to lock your selections.

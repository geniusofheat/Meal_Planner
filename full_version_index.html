Full Version Tools :

Tap a tool title to show its features, then tap a subtitle to review its content. Click the open button to open the tool.

  1. 📋 Grocery List :

    Open !

    Tap the tool title to show its features. Then tap a subtitle to review its content.

    1. Smart Add Tool : +

      Build your grocery list by typing an item name or speaking into the microphone.

      • Type an item name into the text box
      • Speak into the microphone (🎤)
      • Press the add (+) button
      • Remove an item with the ✕ button
    2. Smart Lock : 🔒

      When shopping, check the items you purchase.

      • Check the box beside each item you take
      • Press the lock button to lock your selections
      • Unlock to make changes
    3. Grocery Menu :

      Browse a directory of grocery items within a smart menu.

      • Browse the directory of grocery items
      • Select multiple items without typing
    4. Grocery Menu Smart Search :

      Search the grocery menu for a specific item.

      • Type a name into the text box
      • Speak into the microphone (🎤)
    5. Save Grocery List :

      Keep your list for later and use it with the meal planner.

      • Save the grocery list
      • View your saved lists
      • Pin the list to a specific day in the meal planner to plan a grocery trip

  2. 📖 Cookbook :

    Open !

    Tap the tool title to show its features. Then tap a subtitle to review its content.

    1. Recipe Cards :

      Hundreds of chef-inspired recipes sorted and alphabetized into categories. Each recipe opens as its own card so you keep your place in the menu.

      • Ingredients list
      • Servings
      • Preparation instructions
      • Save button (adds the recipe to your favorites)
      • Plan button (sends the recipe to the meal planner)
      • Back button (returns you to your place in the list)
    2. Favorite Recipes :

      Keep the recipes you love together.

      • Press the save button on any recipe card
      • Saved recipes are added to your favorites list
    3. Meal Planner Access :

      Use cookbook recipes with the meal planner.

      • Press the plan button within each recipe card
      • Choose a day on the monthly calendar
    4. Recipe Creator :

      Create a recipe card with the same layout as the cookbook recipes.

      • Add a recipe with the [ + ] button
      • Accessible only in the cookbook
    5. Recipe Finder :

      The smart search tool finds recipes in the cookbook.

      • Search by recipe name
      • Search by ingredient
      • Use the text box or the microphone

    Open !

    Tap the tool title to show its features. Then tap a subtitle to review its content.

    1. Monthly Calendar :

      Plan a different meal for every day of the month.

      Choose recipes straight from the cookbook or your favorites list.
